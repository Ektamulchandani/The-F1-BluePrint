My Story
Hi, I am Ekta.
I moved from India to the US for my master's in I/O Psychology at the University of New Haven. I landed with no network, no roadmap, and no one who had done exactly what I was trying to do. I had to figure out everything from scratch: how to shortlist programs, how to fund my degree, how to get an internship on CPT, how to convert to full-time on OPT, and how to navigate the H-1B process.
I graduated with a 3.96 GPA and was selected as commencement speaker. I converted my internship into a full-time role at Retensa, an HR technology company, where I now manage 38+ enterprise accounts. I am currently going through the H-1B sponsorship process myself.
I built Reroot because the resource I needed did not exist when I needed it. No institution funded this. I researched everything from scratch, made every mistake, and learned what actually works. This is everything I wish someone had given me on day one, and I am giving it to you for free.
